The routing page groups models by vendor, shows four health metrics as dashboard cards, and filters model IDs and vendors in one row. Each model shows named Providers with OAuth service and account labels, traffic share, and success rate beside its configured Provider weights. Editing a route is a full page instead of a drawer.
